BACKGROUND ACTIVITIES

What do the Occult Club members do at these places?

In general, the background are the locations where the Occult Club will investigate, plan, get together, and look for ghosts. The classroom areas are where they will plan and investigate online. The outdoor areas are mainly of them stalking a ghost in order to find out more in person data about the B.B.G.

Activities done at Each Location

  • A classroom at Frankling Regional School District in Ohio, USA

    • This classroom is where the Occult Club members will be during school hours. Such as talking during school, asking questions about werk, and more average school activities.

  • Game Cycle in Springfield, Ohio, USA

    • The background inspired by Game Cycle will be the location where the Occult Club members will first encounter the B.B.G. and its powers. They will return to this place in hopes to encounter it once more, but the B.B.G. seems to have disappeared from this area.

  • Old and abandoned computer classroom

    • The Occult Club's official club meetings will be held in this classroom. They will do online research about the B.B.G., have discussions about related and unrelated topics, and talk about what their next plan will be.

  • Abandoned Rockefeller skyscraper in Cleveland, Ohio

    • Milo has heard about sightings of an extremely aggressive and irritated ghost and has told the Occult Club about this, which they plan to investigate. They believe that this ghost is somehow connected to the B.B.G., and they go together to investigate and stalk this ghost.

  • Centre City Building (United Brethren Building) in Dayton, Ohio

    • The Occult Club have knows about this aggressive ghost sighting, but they haven't gone because of how infamous the powerful ghost is. There are many rumors about tresspassers disappearing without a single trace left in that building. The Occult Club plans to investigate it, but they are coming properly prepared this time. What they did to prepare and secure their safety is that they worked together and built something that would help them stalk the ghost more efficiently and safely.
