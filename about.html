What is this Game About?

In this game, you are set in Ohio, USA in 2014, and you are in a high school Occult Club. One day, you and your friends have an encounter with a strong ghost with the power to manipulate elctronics. Because of the ghost's fascinating power, your group decides to investigate it, but realize that the B.B.G. is much more powerful and dangerous than they previously believed. The ghost seems to have no clear goal and y'all know practically nothing about the ghost, but it did let you live (by accident or on purpose).
