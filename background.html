Occult Club Background Assets

Game Location

Our game takes place in the Midwest, and takes inspiration primarily from real life locations in Ohio, USA.

Background Inspiration

  • A classroom at Franklin Regional School District in Ohio, USA

    • This classroom was chosen because of its ability to provide a realistic classroom background where the Occult Club members could be during school time

  • Game Cycle in Springfield, Ohio, USA

    • This retro arcade was chosen to allude to POLYBIUS, which was an urban legend about an arcade game that was very addictive, but had horrible side effects like amnesia, seizures, nightmares, and madness. We are alluding to POLYBIUS because the main big bad ghost (B.B.G.) has electrokinesis, which is how they are terrorizing and attacking people.

  • Old and abandoned computer classroom

    • Because of the computers in this classroom, this location is where the Occult Club Members stay afterschool to investigate the B.B.G. and come up with plans.

  • Abandoned Rockefeller skyscraper in Cleveland, Ohio

    • The Rockefeller skyscraper is going to be a place where the Occult Club will go to a ghost that was killed by the B.B.G., which we will stalk in order to find out more information.

  • Centre City Building (United Brethren Building) in Dayton, Ohio

    • The Centre City building has been abandoned for over 20 years, which makes it another great place where a ghost could be located at. Because of its extended vacancy, we decided to have a reason for its abandonment. Due to the mysterious death of this person, there is an aggressive ghost haunting this building, which is why it has stayed abandoned.
