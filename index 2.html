This website is to describe the loccations of where the Occult Club visits throughout the game. This will also explain a brief summary of what they do there, the inspiration of the background, and why we chose this location.

Game Location

  • Our game has backgrounds that take inspiration from real life locations in the Midwest, but the majority of the places are in Ohio.
  • The settings include classroom environments where the Occult Club will plan and discuss, an arcade area as an homage to Polybius, and to real life abandoned places in Ohio where there are aggressive ghosts in the game.
